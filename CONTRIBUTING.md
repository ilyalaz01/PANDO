# Contributing to PANDO

## Current contribution status

PANDO is not accepting external code, documentation, design, schema, prompt, test, or other
copyrightable contributions at this stage. Bug reports and feature discussions are welcome through
the [repository issue tracker](https://github.com/ilyalaz01/PANDO/issues), but do not include
confidential information, third-party proprietary material, personal PANDO data, or substantial
patches in an issue.

Do not open a contribution pull request until the repository publishes a contributor license
agreement (CLA) process and the project owner confirms that the process is active. An unsolicited
pull request may be closed without review or merge.

## Why a CLA is required

PANDO uses a source-available public license and may also be offered under separate commercial
terms. Before an external contribution can be accepted, every contributor must execute a CLA that
gives the project owner sufficient, durable rights to use, reproduce, modify, distribute,
sublicense, and relicense that contribution under both public and commercial terms. A Developer
Certificate of Origin alone is not a substitute for that relicensing permission.

The future CLA must also address:

- contributor identity and authority, including employer authorization where applicable;
- copyright and patent licenses broad enough for public and commercial releases;
- provenance and disclosure of third-party material;
- representations that the contribution can lawfully be submitted;
- whether the contributor retains copyright or assigns it;
- treatment of feedback, confidentiality, warranties, and liability; and
- the exact acceptance record for individual and organizational contributors.

Until that CLA exists, maintainers must not merge an external contribution, even if it appears
small or is submitted under the repository's
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).
Commercial licensing information is in [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md).

This contribution policy is project information, not legal advice. The CLA must be prepared or
reviewed by qualified counsel before the contribution process opens.
